1. Product Vision & Target Business Outcomes

Innovex Command Center is an enterprise-grade, closed-loop AI-Powered Industrial Operations Optimization & Maintenance Intelligence Platform. It bridges raw Internet of Things (IoT) sensor telemetry with executive business decision-making.

Unlike conventional predictive maintenance dashboards that stop at basic graph visualization and raw alert generation, Innovex executes a complete closed-loop decision workflow: detecting multi-sensor anomalies, forecasting Remaining Useful Life (RUL), delivering structured Explainable AI (XAI) root-cause diagnoses, cross-checking spare part lead times against RUL windows, modeling projected financial downtime risks, and automating work order creation.

  • Zero Unplanned Downtime: Eliminate catastrophic machine failures through early multi-sensor anomaly detection and dual-mode RUL forecasting.
  • Stockout Mitigation: Align spare part purchasing lead times with asset degradation curves to ensure zero maintenance delays due to missing components.
  • Financial Risk Transparency: Provide real-time financial impact projections ($/hour downtime risk, SLA penalties, lost throughput) for operational delay scenarios.
  • Operational Efficiency: Reduce Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) by delivering actionable, prescriptive maintenance guidance directly to field technicians.

5. Functional Requirements (FRs)

5.1 FR-1: Dataset Management & Data Quality Portal

  • FR-1.1: Support file uploads in .csv, .xlsx, and .xls formats up to 25MB.
  • FR-1.2: Automated schema detection routing data to modules (inventory, maintenance, logistics, production, reflow).
  • FR-1.3: 0–100 Data Quality Score based on completeness, timestamp continuity, and outlier distribution.
  • FR-1.4: Persistent Delete Dataset capability with confirmation modal, unlinking stored files, purging child records, and toast notifications.

5.2 FR-2: Asset Digital Twin & Telemetry Stream

  • FR-2.1: Render operational Digital Twins for industrial assets (Compressors, Presses, CNC Mills, Robotic Arms).
  • FR-2.2: Display telemetry parameters: Vibration RMS, Thermal Gradient, Line Pressure, Current Variance, Spindle RPM, Load.
  • FR-2.3: Highlight parameters breaching baseline operational envelopes (> ±2.0σ).

5.3 FR-3: Hybrid AI Anomaly Engine & Dual-Mode RUL

  • FR-3.1: Compute composite anomaly score (0–100) using weighted ensemble:
    Composite Score = 0.45 * Model A (Z-Score) + 0.35 * Model B (Isolation Forest) + 0.20 * Model C (Trend)
  • FR-3.2: 42 feature extractions: 3-step rolling means, rolling std dev, RMS ($\sqrt{\text{mean}(x^2)}$), sensor gradients ($\Delta\%/\text{hr}$), and correlation.
  • FR-3.3: Dual-Mode RUL: HistGradientBoostingRegressor (Primary, $\ge 20$ samples) vs Transparent Physical Degradation Rate (Fallback, $< 20$ samples).

5.4 FR-4: Structured Explainable AI (XAI) & Prescriptive Actions

  • FR-4.1: Non-black-box root causes: What Happened, Why Happened (breached sensor metrics), Failure Probability (%), Risk If Delayed.
  • FR-4.2: Step-by-step prescriptive maintenance instructions.

5.5 FR-5: Spare Parts Inventory Matching & Readiness

  • FR-5.1: Match equipment components to live spare parts inventory records.
  • FR-5.2: Evaluate Readiness Status: READY (lead time $\le$ RUL), AT RISK (lead time approaches RUL), UNAVAILABLE (lead time > RUL).

5.6 FR-6: Maintenance Scheduling & Work Orders

  • FR-6.1: 1-click work order creation directly from AI diagnostic cards into the Maintenance Calendar.
  • FR-6.2: Interactive calendar with filtering by priority, status, and technician.
  • FR-6.3: Dynamic overdue calculation based on system time vs scheduled date.

5.7 FR-7: What-If Financial Risk & Scenario Simulator

  • FR-7.1: Simulate operational decisions (maintenance delay hours, procurement price surges, production adjustments).
  • FR-7.2: Compare Baseline vs Simulated metrics across downtime cost, production loss, SLA penalties, and repair expense.
  • FR-7.3: Display Financial Risk Delta (e.g., "+$18,400 Financial Risk Delta if delayed 24h").

6. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

  • Latency: Analytical execution $\le 350\text{ms}$; API response $\le 50\text{ms}$; UI render $\le 100\text{ms}$.
  • High Availability: Automatic zero-downtime failover to Enterprise In-Memory Data Store upon MongoDB cloud disconnection.
  • Security: JWT token authentication, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), audit logging engine.
